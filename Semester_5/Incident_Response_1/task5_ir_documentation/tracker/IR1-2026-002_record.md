## Summary

Tara Jones (test.nurse) moved Nurse -> Billing (CHG-2026-0201), but the host kept her clinical group. She read synthetic record PT-1003, staged a copy, started an uploader (syncd.py) posting it to 10.78.99.66:8080 every 5 s, and added a cron job to restart it. Wazuh raised rule 100221 within seconds and containment started at the alert. 10 uploads reached the drop site before egress was blocked.
