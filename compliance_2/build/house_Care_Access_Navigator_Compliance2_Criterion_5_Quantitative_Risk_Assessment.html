5. Quantitative Risk Assessment

5.1 Method

FAIR-style Monte Carlo simulation (analysis/risk_monte_carlo.py, 100,000 simulated years, seed 42). For each capstone risk: annual loss = sum of N loss events, where N is Poisson with a rate drawn from a triangular distribution, and each event's loss is drawn from a PERT distribution. This builds on Risk Management 1, which used single-point ALE (e.g. asset $100,000 × EF 0.30 = SLE $30,000; ARO 0.5 → ALE $15,000) and a business impact analysis (Keycloak IAM outage $25,000 for the flagship). Simulation replaces single points with ranges and gives percentiles, not just an average.

Insurance premiums are priced at expected covered loss × 1.35 (insurer expenses and profit), so insurance alone costs more than it saves on average; its value is cutting the tail. Option C lowers both the average and the tail, because the extra control shrinks what the insurer has to cover.

Residual risk management plan: (1) Board accepts option C and the appetite statement; (2) FIDO2 rollout in the 2028 roadmap, insurance bound for 2027; (3) R02 stays residual Moderate through JML automation (CAN-FIND-001 automatic deprovisioning closes the last gap); (4) triggers for re-assessment: scenario S1 or S3 conditions, a SEV-1 incident, or the model's P95 moving above $5M; (5) the model is re-run each quarter with updated register scores from CISO Assistant.
