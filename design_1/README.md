## Purpose statement

> MoodBuddy is a simple Python mood tracker where a user names one of six moods and gets an age-appropriate
> message and activity in under a minute. It is being made so busy students, families and older adults
> (ages 4 to 100) can build a daily habit of naming their feelings and notice repeating negative moods early.
